Coastal Energy Consortium: Annual Review 2023

The Coastal Energy Consortium brings together nine organisations working on marine renewable energy, including Meridian Tidal Power, Westcoast Grid, the University of Calder, Brightwater Composites and Aurelia Offshore Wind. The consortium is chaired by Priya Raman of Halcyon Ventures. Because Raman also sits on the board of Meridian Tidal Power, she recused herself from the vote that awarded funding to the Saltmarsh Array.

Blue Transition Fund. In 2023 the consortium's Blue Transition Fund distributed 22 million euros across five projects. The largest award, 9 million euros, went to Aurelia Offshore Wind for a floating wind demonstrator at Corran Bank. The Saltmarsh Array tidal trial run by Meridian Tidal Power received 6.5 million euros. The University of Calder received 3 million euros to upgrade its wave tank. Westcoast Grid received 2.5 million euros for a battery storage pilot at the Dunmore substation. Community energy cooperatives received the remaining 1 million euros.

Supply chain quality. The blade delamination found during the Saltmarsh trial prompted the consortium to adopt a shared quality standard for composite components, called CEC-QS1, in November 2023. All members that buy or make composite blades and floats have agreed to require batch-level testing under CEC-QS1, and Brightwater Composites was the first supplier certified against it.

Grid constraints. The Dunmore substation, operated by Westcoast Grid, is close to its export limit. Westcoast Grid has said that the substation upgrade needed for Saltmarsh Phase II cannot be completed before 2025, which delays Phase II until then. The battery storage pilot is intended to relieve some of the constraint in the meantime.

Wildlife. Members agreed to adopt the passive acoustic monitoring protocol developed by Dr. Lena Fischbach's team during the Saltmarsh trial as the consortium's common method for monitoring marine mammals, so that results from different sites can be compared.

Outlook. Priorities for 2024 are the Dunmore grid upgrade, certification of more suppliers under CEC-QS1, and a second funding call focused on floating wind and tidal arrays.
