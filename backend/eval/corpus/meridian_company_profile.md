Meridian Tidal Power: Company Profile

Meridian Tidal Power is a tidal energy developer headquartered in Port Ellery. The company was founded in 2014 by Ingrid Vasquez-Hale and Tomasz Okonkwo. Vasquez-Hale, the chief executive officer, previously spent nine years as a turbine engineer at Norrland Marine, where she worked on subsea drivetrains. Okonkwo, the chief technology officer, holds a PhD in fluid dynamics from the University of Calder, where his thesis modelled turbulent flow around rotating blades in shallow channels.

Meridian's flagship product is the Kestrel-2, a horizontal-axis tidal stream turbine with a rated capacity of 1.2 megawatts. The Kestrel-2 sits on a gravity-base foundation and uses a direct-drive generator, which removes the gearbox that caused most failures in earlier tidal designs. Its three composite blades are manufactured by Brightwater Composites under a supply agreement signed in 2018. Final assembly takes place at Meridian's manufacturing facility at the Dunmore yard, a former shipbuilding site.

In 2019 Meridian raised a Series B round of 48 million euros. The round was led by Halcyon Ventures, a climate technology investor. As part of the investment, Priya Raman, a partner at Halcyon Ventures, joined the Meridian board of directors. The funds were used to finish the Kestrel-2 design and to prepare the company's first multi-turbine array.

In 2021 Meridian hired Dr. Lena Fischbach as head of environmental monitoring. Fischbach previously led the marine acoustics group at the Calder Marine Institute, where she developed passive acoustic methods for tracking harbour porpoises. Her team is responsible for the wildlife monitoring that regulators require before and after any turbine deployment.

Meridian does not own transmission infrastructure. Power from its arrays is exported through a subsea cable to the onshore substation at Dunmore, which is operated by Westcoast Grid under a connection agreement signed in 2020. Meridian is a founding member of the Coastal Energy Consortium, an industry and research group for marine renewable energy.
